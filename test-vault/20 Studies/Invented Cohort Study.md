# Invented cohort study

**Invented.** The study every other fixture links to.

`governance.identifiers` is §5.1's field with §5.1's vocabulary — the same block a
request carries, one level up. A request says what *that request* asks for; the
study says what the ethics approval permits. D2 checks one against the other.

`indirect` here means a form on this study may collect indirect identifiers and
must justify each one, and may not collect direct ones.
