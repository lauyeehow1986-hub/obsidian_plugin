# 1 Purpose

Invented. This note is deliberately **bare**: it declares nothing under
`governs:`, nothing has declared itself derived from it, it has never been
frozen, and it has no `review_due`.

That is the fixture. The register reports all three as findings rather than
leaving them as an absence — a policy nothing declares a dependency on is one
whose revision would produce an empty impact map, and a policy never frozen is
one whose first revision has nothing to diff against.

# 2 Retention periods

Research records are retained for the period stated in the approved protocol,
and for no shorter a period than the funder requires.
