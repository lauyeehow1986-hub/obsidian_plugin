Fixture note. A one-off with no recurrence rule, so it never lapses: once the
date passes it becomes history rather than an alarm. That distinction is why
there are two note types — only an `obligation` lapses.

`consequence` is not required here. §5.7 attaches it to obligations, and
demanding a paragraph about a one-off deadline would only teach people to type
"n/a". Everything here is invented.
